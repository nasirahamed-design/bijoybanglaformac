Type Bangla on your Mac

Use the familiar Bijoy keyboard layout to type Unicode Bangla in any app.

  • Adds Bijoy Unicode and Bijoy Classic (SutonnyMJ) to the input menu.
  • Switch input sources with Control-Space or the Globe key.
  • Works with macOS 13 Ventura or later on Apple silicon and Intel.
One final step

After installation, log out and back in so macOS can load the new keyboard.
