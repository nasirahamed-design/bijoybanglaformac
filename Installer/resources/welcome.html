Bijoy Bangla বিজয় বাংলা

Type Unicode Bangla on your Mac with the Bijoy keyboard layout, in every app.

  • Adds বাংলা (বিজয়) and English to the input menu in the top bar
  • Switch with Control-Space or the 🌐 key
  • Works with macOS 13 Ventura or later, Apple Silicon and Intel

At the end you will be asked to log out. macOS loads a new keyboard when you log back in.
